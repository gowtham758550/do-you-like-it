Do You Like It? ❤️

Privacy policy · Last updated 23 November 2026 (version 0.2.2)

Do You Like It? is a browser extension that hides prices on shopping websites. In short, it does not collect, send or sell any information about you.

What the extension stores

Three things, all kept only in your own browser using Chrome's local extension storage:

  • Whether price hiding is on or off.
  • Which store groups you've folded in the picks list.
  • The products you mark with ♡ Like: the product’s name, image link, price and currency, page link, the shop it came from, and when you liked it.

This information never leaves your device. It is not synced to your Google account and is not sent to us or anyone else. Removing a product from the list, or uninstalling the extension, deletes it.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect personal information such as your name, email, address, location or payment details.
  • It does not track the websites you visit or your browsing history.
  • It does not use analytics, cookies, advertising or third-party services.
  • It does not send any data over the network.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Which websites it runs on

The extension supports six shopping sites: Amazon, eBay, Etsy, IKEA, Flipkart and Myntra. Amazon covers India, the US, UK, Germany, Canada, Australia and the UAE; eBay covers the US, UK, Germany, Australia and Canada. Etsy and IKEA are supported on their international .com storefronts. Amazon.in, Flipkart and Myntra are enabled at install; other store permissions are optional and Chrome asks before they are enabled. On approved pages, the extension reads page content only to hide prices and add Like buttons, inside your browser.

When you open its toolbar popup, the extension reads the current tab's address to offer turning it on for that store. It does not keep or send that address.
