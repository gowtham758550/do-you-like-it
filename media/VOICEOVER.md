# Demo video: voiceover script

`do-you-like-it-demo-4k.mp4`: 3840×2160, 30 fps, 55 seconds, no audio. Products: armchairs on Amazon, earrings on Myntra, sofas on Flipkart.

Each line is roughly as long as its scene at a relaxed pace. Change the wording freely. If a line runs long, the next scene's first second or two is a quiet hold, so you have slack.

## Recording tips

- Record in a quiet room with a phone or headset mic held about a fist's width from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
- Record the whole script in one take, then line it up with the video in your editor. Shifting the audio a few hundred milliseconds is fine.
- iMovie or CapCut both work: drop in the MP4, add your audio track, and export at 4K.
